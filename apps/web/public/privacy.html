Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · This page is the whole policy.

Short version

Only Inbox has no account system, no server and no analytics. It does not collect, store or transmit your messages, your password, your session or your contacts. The only data it keeps is a handful of on/off switches, stored on your own device.

What the app and extension can see

Only Inbox works by changing the Instagram page you are already looking at. On iOS the app displays instagram.com in a single web view; in a browser the extension runs only on instagram.com. Because of that it can see the page, in the same way the page is visible to you.

It uses that access for exactly three things: deciding whether a URL is one of the small set we allow, hiding interface elements that lead somewhere else, and recolouring the accent colour.

What it never does

  • It never reads, stores or transmits your password or your cookies.
  • It never sends the content of a message, a profile or a thread anywhere.
  • It never automates activity: no auto-likes, auto-follows, scheduled posts or mass messages.
  • It never injects remote code. An optional selector list is plain data (JSON) and cannot execute.
  • It contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no crash reporting service.

What is stored, and where

Settings only — for example whether stories are allowed, or whether a reel a friend sent may be opened. They live in the extension's local storage in your browser, or in the app's own storage on your phone. Deleting the app or the extension deletes them.

Your Instagram session is held by Safari, Chrome or the app's web view, exactly as a normal browser holds it. Only Inbox does not have its own copy.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no fonts loaded from a third party and no third-party scripts. It is static HTML, CSS and one small local script. Your browser tells the script its user agent so that the right install option can be highlighted; that value stays in your browser.

Children

Only Inbox is not directed at children under 13, and it cannot be used without an Instagram account, which has its own minimum age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to migrate or delete on our side.
