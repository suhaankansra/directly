The whole product

Four tabs. One reel. No feed.

Most apps hide their limits in a settings screen. This page is the opposite: here is everything Directly does, everything it refuses to do, and the handful of switches you actually control.

The four tabs

Instagram's own pages, re-navigated. Nothing is rebuilt, so nothing looks foreign.

Tab one

DMs

Your inbox, threads, message requests and notes. Sending text, photos, voice notes, stickers and reactions all work, because it is Instagram's own composer.

Tab two

Activity

Likes, comments, follows and requests for you. Follow Back works; tapping through to the post it refers to does not jump you into a feed.

Tab three

Profile

Anyone you look up on purpose. Searching by name means typing a name you already know — not being handed a grid to browse.

Tab four

Lock

A native screen, not an Instagram page: the switches on this page, your signed-in account, and sign out. It exists so the other three can stay small.

The one-reel rule

A friend sends you a reel. You should be able to watch it — and then be finished. That is the single hardest thing to get right, so it is worth stating precisely.

✓ What happens

  • Opening a reel from a DM pins that one id
  • The clip plays, and loops when it ends
  • The next and previous arrows are removed
  • The slides either side of it are hidden, so a swipe has nowhere to land
  • Back returns you to the conversation you came from

✕ What does not

  • Swiping to the next reel
  • Opening a different reel while one is pinned
  • Landing on the Reels tab itself, from anywhere
  • Reaching a reel through a link that did not come from a DM or a profile

Instagram uses both /reel/<code>/ and /reels/<code>/ for a single clip, so both are treated as one item. The Reels tab and audio pages stay blocked either way.

What gets blocked, and why

Blocking is by route, not by hiding things and hoping. Every one of these lands you back in your inbox.

The home feed

The infinite one. There is no version of it that ends, which is the whole reason this exists.

Reels and audio pages

A reel plays once, on its own, from a DM. The tab that serves you an endless sequence does not open.

Explore and search results

Searching for a person is allowed. Browsing what Instagram suggests you look at is not.

Stories

Off by default, because a story session is tap-after-tap. There is a switch if you decide otherwise.

Sign-up flows

You already have an account. New-account and sign-up routes stay shut so a mistap cannot start one.

Links that lead into the above

A share in a DM that points at the feed, a tag page or a reel you were not sent is not clickable.

The switches you do get

Small, specific, and all off-by-default toward the stricter setting where it matters.

1

Stories

Off by default. Turn it on if stories are how your people actually talk.

2

Reels from DMs

On by default. Turn it off and the one-reel rule becomes no-reels-at-all.

3

Labels and the block note

Icon-only navigation if you want the bar quieter, and the short "not here" note can go too.

No account. No server. No copy of anything.

Directly never sees your password and keeps no session of its own — sign-in happens inside Instagram's page, and the cookies stay in your browser or your phone's web view. There is nothing to sign up for because there is nothing to sign up to.

Get it

What it cannot do

Worth knowing before you install it, rather than after.

It cannot run inside a phone browser on Android

Chrome for Android does not support extensions, and a website cannot modify Instagram's pages from the outside. The iOS app and the desktop extension are the two honest shapes.

It cannot promise Instagram never changes the page

Instagram ships markup changes constantly. Blocking fails closed — you land in your inbox rather than the feed — while cosmetic hiding fails open, which is ugly but usable.

It cannot stop you opening Instagram elsewhere

There is no lock on your account and no password on the app. It is a shape for the app you already use, not a parental control.
