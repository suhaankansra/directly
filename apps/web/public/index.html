✕ What is gone

  • The home feed
  • The Reels tab, and the next reel after this one
  • Explore, search results and suggested grids
  • Stories, unless you turn them back on
  • Any link in a DM that points into the above

How it works

Directly does not copy Instagram and does not touch your account. It shows you Instagram's own pages, in your own browser or in a single web view on your phone, and hides the parts that lead somewhere endless.

1

You sign in to Instagram

Inside your browser or the app. We never see the password, and the session stays in the browser's own cookie store.

2

It opens your DMs

Every other entry point goes back to your inbox, with a short note explaining why.

3

One item, then a full stop

A reel a friend sent plays, loops and cannot scroll onward. Back returns you to the conversation.

Straight answers

Does this read my messages?

No. It changes what Instagram shows you — hiding elements, moving you back to your inbox — and it has no network code of its own. There is no account, no analytics and no server to send anything to.

Is this allowed?

Directly does not automate anything: you type every message yourself, and no unofficial API is used. It does modify a page you are looking at, which is what ad-blockers and reading modes have always done. Check Instagram's terms yourself — this page is not legal advice.

What happens when Instagram changes?

Blocking fails closed: if a rule stops matching, you land in your inbox rather than the feed. Cosmetic rules fail open, which is ugly but usable. Selector lists can be updated as data, without a new version of the app.

Can I get the feed back for a minute?

That is the point of the product, so there is no switch for it. You can always open Instagram in a normal browser window — Directly is not a lock on your account.
