Privacy

Plain language, no asterisks.

What this extension can see

It runs only on instagram.com, because that is where it has to change the page. It can therefore see the page in the same way your browser shows it to you.

What it never does

  • It never reads or stores your password, your cookies or your session.
  • It never sends your messages, DMs, profile or browsing anywhere.
  • It has no server, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking pixels.
  • It does not automate anything on Instagram: no auto-likes, follows or messages.

What it stores

Six on/off switches, kept in your browser's own extension storage on this device. That is the entire database. Clearing the extension's data erases it.

What it changes

It hides Instagram's navigation, the feed, Explore, Reels and the next-reel swipe, and it recolours the accent to purple. Everything you send is typed by you, in Instagram's own page, through Instagram's own code.
