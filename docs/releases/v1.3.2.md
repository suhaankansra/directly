Directly opens Instagram straight into your DMs and takes away the feed, Reels and Explore. Watch the
reel a friend sent, then stop.

A follow-up to 1.3.1, which fixed the first Facebook redirect but not the second one.

**The browser opening by itself.** Every navigation the page started was weighed the same way: a page
whose address is not `www.instagram.com` was handed to Safari. But a *subframe* is not the page you are
looking at — Instagram loads its Meta pixel, `facebook.com/tr`, in one on nearly every screen. That was
the redirect that arrived with no tap at all: the DMs you were reading stayed put and Safari appeared
with a Facebook address. Subframes are now left alone, so only the page itself is judged, and only the
page itself can ever leave the app.

**Facebook, anywhere, now stays off Safari entirely.** Whatever the address — the OAuth dialog,
`login.php`, a plain `facebook.com` link in a conversation — handing it to the browser signs you in to
a cookie store this app cannot read, which is what made "Continue with Facebook" look dead and tapping
it again loop. On the sign-in screen the app now says Facebook sign-in cannot finish here and to use
your username and password; on any other screen it says it does not open Facebook pages. Nothing
Facebook is loaded in the web view either: the route rules redirect with a relative path, and running
them on `facebook.com` would land there instead of on Instagram.

**The Profile tab finds your handle now.** 1.3.1 asked the page for your profile link, which worked
only when your avatar was on screen — and the inbox, a thread and a story do not render it. It now also
reads Instagram's own navigation, whose **Profile** item links to your handle on every signed-in
screen. That is an `aria-label`, not a layout position, so it survives both Instagram layouts.

**A tab that Instagram refuses says so.** Activity is served at `/accounts/activity/`, and when
Instagram redirects that to the home feed the route guard does its job and puts you back in the inbox
— which looked identical to a dead tab. A tap that lands back on the inbox now says
`Instagram doesn't serve Activity here` (or `Couldn't open your profile`) instead of moving the
highlight and nothing else. If Activity opens normally, that is the answer too: this release is how we
find out which one it does on your phone.

## What this build cannot do

- **Facebook sign-in.** Not a limitation Directly can lift: the session would have to land in Safari's
  cookie store, which this app never sees. Use your Instagram username and password.
- **No alerts while the app is closed.** A `WKWebView` cannot receive web push, so a banner appears
  only while Directly is running. Background alerts would mean a server holding your session.
- **No instant switching between stored accounts.** One web view, one cookie jar, one session.

## Android

1. Download the APK on the phone, or transfer it there.
2. Allow installs from the browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Install. Package `com.directly.app`, version 1.3.2, versionCode 6, target SDK 36.

Signed with the Android debug key, which is what makes sideloading possible. That is not a Play Store
upload key, so this build will never arrive through the Play Store.

## iOS

The IPA is built with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`: a real `arm64` device binary with no signature,
because a certificate does not belong in CI. iOS refuses to install it until it carries a signature
from a team the device trusts.

**A. Sign it on your Mac** — needs a Development or Ad Hoc profile that already lists the device:

```bash
tools/resign-ipa.sh ~/Downloads/directly-v1.3.2-unsigned.ipa \
  --profile ~/Downloads/Directly.mobileprovision \
  --device 00008110-000A1B2C3D4E5F60
```

It checks the bundle id, team, profile expiry and the device UDID against the profile before touching
anything, signs nested code deepest-first, verifies each item and the finished bundle, then installs
with `devicectl` (Developer Mode must be on). `--dry-run` prints the plan and signs nothing.

**B. Sideloadly or AltStore** — drag the IPA in, enter your Apple ID, choose your team. A free
account's profile expires after 7 days, after which the app refuses to launch until it is signed
again; AltStore can refresh on a schedule, Sideloadly cannot.

## Notes

- The subframe fix is a fact about the code, not a reproduction: `isTopFrame` was in every navigation
  request and was never checked, so a pixel iframe and a link you tapped were treated as the same
  thing. Unit tests cover the classification and the profile-link sources; the iframe path needs a
  device with a session to watch, which is why the address bar report that named Safari was the useful
  part of the last one.
- The DM badge and the alert are local. Nothing arrives while the app is closed.
- A reel opened from a conversation stays on that reel; swiping up or pressing next does nothing.
- Independent project, not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
